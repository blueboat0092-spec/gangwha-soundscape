ABOUT
Ganghwa Island is the place where I spent the most important years of my childhood.

Living in a dormitory back then, Ganghwa felt like a boring rural island, and all I wanted was to escape to the bustling city of Seoul on weekends.

Later in my twenties, I moved to the city to pursue music, playing in bands, but I lost my own rhythm amidst the fast pace of urban life.

When I felt I could no longer endure it, I left everything behind and fled back here to Ganghwa.

Returning to the island, I recorded the deep evening bells of Jeondeungsa temple harmonizing with insects, the Angelus bell of Onsuri church, and the changing faces of the sea shaped by the tides.

This process became a time of reflection and healing for my weary mind.
